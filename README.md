# サクスピ Saku Speakのサイト

Saku SpeakのiPhoneアプリ紹介、サポート、プライバシーの静的サイトです。GitHub Pagesは `main` のルートを公開元にします。

- [アプリ紹介](https://ki-84.github.io/saku-speak-support/)
- [サポート](https://ki-84.github.io/saku-speak-support/support.html)
- [プライバシー](https://ki-84.github.io/saku-speak-support/privacy.html)

[サクスピのカタカナロゴ](assets/sakusupi-wordmark.svg) と [Sの吹き出しマーク](assets/saku-mark.svg) はSVGです。iPhoneのアイコンとWebのタッチアイコンは同じ吹き出しマークを使います。見出し用のフォントは日本語明朝体 Noto Serif JP のサブセットです。 [ライセンス](assets/serif-OFL.txt)

App Store公開時は、`index.html` に日本のストアのアプリURLと確定価格を掲載します。サポートページにはApp Store Connectへの提出前に販売者の公開連絡先を追加します。

## トップの写真

ImageGenの組み込みツールで生成した写真をWebPに書き出しました。 [商談の写真](assets/meeting-editorial.webp)

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: landscape editorial hero photograph for Saku Speak, a Japanese iPhone app for speaking English at work
Primary request: produce one authentic editorial photograph of a small cross-border business meeting in Tokyo, capturing the pause as a Japanese professional listens to a follow-up question and considers a reply.
Scene/backdrop: quiet contemporary meeting room with dark green-tinted glass, daylight from a large window, and a restrained walnut table. Honest, lived-in materials and slight imperfections.
Subject: the main subject is a Japanese woman in her mid to late 30s with shoulder-length dark hair, wearing a well-cut navy jacket over a simple warm gray top. She is seated, attentive and composed, a little thoughtful, naturally looking toward the person speaking rather than the camera. A single foreign colleague is visible only from behind or softly out-of-focus in the left foreground. Hands, if visible, rest naturally on the table near a closed notebook.
Style and medium: a photograph from an independent business magazine, shot on a 50mm lens at seated eye level, subtly textured documentary realism, natural skin and ordinary real-world gestures. Slight film grain and rich shadow detail.
Composition: landscape roughly 4:3 with the woman at x=62%, face in the upper-middle, both faces and important detail safely inside the central 75%. Room edges, window reflection and the second person add depth, with enough context left around the subject for multiple responsive crops. No overlay space required.
Lighting and colors: soft directional natural daylight; muted ink blue, deep olive green, warm neutral skin, and gray wood. Fine contrast and no artificial studio glow.
Avoid: stock-photo smiles, looking at the camera, dramatic color grading, glossy AI skin, elaborate accessories, corporate logos, legible words, text overlay, phone mockups, and distorted fingers.
```
